# Code of conduct

This project is a technical artifact. Participation is open to anyone who
wants to improve it, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
employer. The standard is simple: be precise about the work and decent to the
people doing it.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, code, and evidence, not people.
- State assumptions and cite sources when you disagree.
- Assume good faith, but ask for the missing operating point.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that does not fit the
  artifact's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith repetition.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Using the project to advertise unrelated products or services.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, and any project space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through GitHub. If the
report involves a maintainer, use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) channel instead.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may remove comments, close
threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation. They will avoid
public disclosure of the reporter's identity. Enforcement decisions are
proportional to the behavior and its impact on the project.
