Why an iframe

The console keeps its own state.

The real application owns its route, theme, saved game progress, and calculators. Loading it directly means what you try here behaves exactly like the standalone tool, including the parts a static screenshot cannot show.

Executable evidence

Every claim drawer resolves a path into results/results.json. The verification modal compares 34,650 fields with zero relative difference.
